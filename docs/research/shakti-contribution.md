## 1. Research Question and Target Audience

How can college students find suitable tutors and request tutoring sessions with less uncertainty and effort?

Our proposed audience is college students seeking academic support in specific subjects. The goal of TutorConnect is to simplify the process of finding a tutor, comparing available options, and requesting tutoring sessions.

This target audience is a research hypothesis rather than a validated customer segment.

## 2. Competitor Research

### Wyzant

**Source:** https://support.wyzant.com/students-parents/find-a-tutor/how-does-wyzant-work/

Wyzant describes tutor searching, profiles, qualifications, reviews, messaging, and scheduling options.

**Design implication:** TutorConnect could display tutor expertise, availability, and relevant qualifications so students can compare tutors before requesting a session.

### Varsity Tutors

**Source:** https://www.varsitytutors.com/how-we-operate

Varsity Tutors describes guided tutor matching and online learning support.

**Design implication:** TutorConnect could consider guided matching as a future feature for students who are unsure which tutor would best fit their needs.

### Tutor.com

**Source:** https://www.tutor.com/FAQ

Tutor.com describes online tutoring services with scheduled and on-demand options.

**Design implication:** TutorConnect should clearly communicate appointment availability and booking status so students understand what happens after requesting a session.

These observations come from the companies' published descriptions and do not establish which platform students prefer.

## 4. Hypothetical Student Personas

### Persona A — Busy College Student

A college student balancing classes, assignments, and other responsibilities who may need tutoring before an upcoming examination.

**Potential needs:**
- Quickly finding tutors for specific subjects.
- Viewing relevant qualifications.
- Comparing available appointment times.
- Understanding whether a session is confirmed.

**Assumption to test:** Subject filtering and clear scheduling information may reduce uncertainty when searching for tutoring.

### Persona B — Returning Learner

A college student who regularly seeks academic assistance and may prefer scheduling multiple sessions with the same tutor.

**Potential needs:**
- Finding a familiar tutor.
- Maintaining consistent scheduling.
- Comparing tutors based on subject expertise.
- Understanding the next steps after requesting a session.

**Assumption to test:** Students seeking repeated academic support may value tutor continuity.

Both personas are hypothetical. No real student interviews have been conducted.

## 5. Two Alternative Design Approaches

### Approach A — Search-First

Students select a subject, compare tutor profiles, and request a tutoring session.

**Advantages:**
- Provides students with control over tutor selection.
- Makes qualifications and availability visible.
- Supports comparison between tutors.

**Potential limitation:** Students may spend additional time comparing options.

### Approach B — Guided Matching

Students describe their academic needs and receive a tutor recommendation.

**Advantages:**
- Could simplify tutor selection.
- May assist students unfamiliar with tutoring services.

**Potential limitation:** Requires additional matching logic and clear explanations of how recommendations are generated.

**Proposed direction:** Begin with a search-first approach using subject filtering, tutor profiles, and a clear session-request process. This is a design proposal, not a validated student preference.

## 6. Proposed User Workflow

1. A student selects the subject in which they need tutoring.
2. The student compares available tutor profiles.
3. The student chooses a tutor and requests a session.
4. The system displays the request as pending.
5. The appointment becomes confirmed only after the tutor accepts.

The workflow is a proposed design and has not been implemented.
